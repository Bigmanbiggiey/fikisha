# Brand Name

**Status:** DECIDED — **`Fikisha`** (founder, 2026-09-08). Round 1 (`Mzigo`) was
rejected; `Fikisha` was chosen from the round-2 shortlist below. Remaining work
is the clearance/registration steps in §4 (BRS name reservation, KIPI trade-mark,
domains/handles, informal-use check). The brand is applied as configuration
(labels, logo, domain), **not a Phase 1 blocker**. "Logistix" may survive as a
legal-entity / holding name if desired.

The shortlist and rationale below are retained for the record.

---

## 1. Selection criteria (unchanged)

The name will be spoken on phone calls between operators and businesses in
Kitengela and its environs, typed by low-literacy users, and printed on
receipts. It should:

1. Evoke the service in the local language context (English + Swahili + Sheng),
   without being a bare dictionary label.
2. Be short (2 syllables ideal, 3 max) and easy to pronounce and spell.
3. Not lock the brand to one vehicle class (fits a boda parcel and a trailer
   haul equally).
4. Work as a verb / everyday phrase.
5. Be legally clearable — distinct enough to reserve at BRS and register at KIPI,
   with an available domain and handles.
6. Not collide with an active Kenyan logistics brand (Lori, Kobo360, LoadLink,
   Leta, Amitruck, Sendy's residual recognition, etc.).

## 2. Revised shortlist

| Name | Origin / meaning | Why it works | Watch-outs |
|------|------------------|--------------|------------|
| **Tuma** *(recommended)* | Swahili imperative **"send"**. Every Kenyan already says *"tuma pesa"* on M-Pesa — the instinct to *send* transfers cleanly to sending goods. | Ultra-short (2 syllables); verb-native ("Tuma it", *"nimetuma mzigo kwa Tuma"*); pan-Kenyan, not just Kitengela; modern, fintech-adjacent feel that signals trust and tracking; not tied to any vehicle | "Tuma" is a common word — needs a distinctive word-mark/logo for trade-mark strength; **clearance search essential** (possible fintech/remittance uses); confirm `.co.ke` / handles |
| **Fikisha** | Swahili **"deliver / cause to arrive"** — the actual delivery verb. | Outcome-focused (the goods *arrive*); more ownable as a mark than a bare noun; clear meaning to operators and businesses; *"tunafikisha mzigo wako"* | 3 syllables; slightly harder to type than Tuma; check for existing courier use |
| **Relay** | English — goods passed **hand to hand** along a chain. | Directly evokes the platform's core: an auditable **chain of custody**; short, modern, easy; works in English-language marketing and contracts | English-only (less local warmth); **trade-mark risk** — "Relay" is used by delivery/freight-payments firms elsewhere; needs a proper KIPI + international-conflict check |
| **Boma** | Swahili / Maa — a **homestead / livestock enclosure / yard**. Strong resonance in **Maasai-majority Kajiado**, and it maps onto the operator **yard/base/stage** the product is built around. | Short, warm, locally rooted; ties the brand to the "build around the stage/base" principle; memorable | Used by other Kenyan businesses (The BOMA Project NGO; Boma Hotels; Boma International Hospitality College) — **conflict search needed**; less obviously about moving goods, so needs a tagline |
| **Njia** | Swahili **"way / route / path"**. | Neutral, safe, brandable; evokes movement and connection without cargo- or vehicle-specificity; easy to say | Abstract — needs a tagline to land ("Njia — moving goods, the trusted way"); check for transport/mapping uses |

Set aside from round 1: `Mzigo` (rejected), `Beba` (past defunct-product
associations, e.g. BebaPay), `Kituo` (kept only as a concept, weaker recall),
`Leta` (conflicts with an existing Kenyan logistics/procurement startup), `Lori`
(too close to Lori Systems and the generic word for lorry).

---

## 3. Selection

**Chosen: `Fikisha`** (founder, 2026-09-08) — Swahili for *"deliver / get it
there"*, the delivery verb itself. Outcome-focused, meaningful to every operator
and business in the pilot area, and more ownable as a trade mark than a bare
common noun. Suggested tagline direction: *"Fikisha — mzigo wako, mikono salama"*
("your load, safe hands") or English *"Fikisha — delivered, tracked and
trusted."*

(Round-2 shortlist for the record: `Tuma`, `Fikisha` ✓, `Relay`, `Boma`, `Njia`.)

**Legal-entity vs consumer-brand:** the company can be incorporated under a plain
name (e.g. "Fikisha Technologies Limited" or a neutral holding name) while the
app brand is `Fikisha` — a normal split that preserves flexibility.
