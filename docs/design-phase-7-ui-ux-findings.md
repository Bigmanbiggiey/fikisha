## 1. How this was done

- **Capture:** a headless Playwright script (not committed; brief D-6 as
  amended) signed in as seeded dev accounts, one per role:
  - business owner, Kitengela Traders
  - operator/driver
  - Operations Officer
  - no account: public pages and the recipient scoped link

  It screenshotted 38 screens at **360 / 768 / 1280 px** in **English and
  Swahili** (228 images). Navigation was client-side, with no reloads.
- **Automated checks per screen:**
  - axe-core, WCAG 2.0/2.1/2.2 A+AA, at 1280/en and 360/sw
  - horizontal-overflow measurement at every width
  - console errors and failed API calls
- **Code map:** grep inventory of raw form controls, arbitrary Tailwind
  values, breakpoint usage, date formatting, hardcoded strings and heading
  structure.
- **Review:** screenshots and code checked against the approved baseline
  (brief §4). Every finding cites the section it breaks.

**Coverage gaps (honest limits):**
- **Exception states** (P3 §22: offline, error, expired link, stale,
  unauthorised) were **not** systematically captured. The seeded data
  reaches only the happy-path states plus cancelled, completed and disputed.
- **Increment 8 staff interactions** (cancel modal, contact reveal,
  high-value modal) were captured in their resting state only.

Both gaps should be closed in the Increment 10 build pass.

**What already works well (keep):**
- The Ops monitor table at desktop width.
- The recipient view: minimum disclosure, clear, Swahili reads naturally.
- Status chips always carry icon + text.
- The timeline's done/current/upcoming treatment.
- The Stage & Yard palette reads calmly and consistently.
- axe found **no** violations on any business screen.

## 2. Summary

| Severity | Count | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Blocker** | 3 | A user can't read or complete something important, or an irreversible action has no safeguard |
| **Major** | 10 | Clear break from the approved baseline, or a significant UX or accessibility problem |
| **Minor** | 4 | Real, but low impact |
| **Polish** | 1 | Cosmetic |

**Three systemic causes account for most of it:**
1. **The `sm:` breakpoint is misused** (F-01). This one config/usage
   mismatch breaks every multi-column list on phones.
2. **The role-aware shell from P1 and P3 §24.2 was never built** (F-04). Every
   role shares one flat, wrapping top bar, and the driver shares the Business
   Job Detail (F-03).
3. **Shared components carry English defaults** (F-05). Swahili isn't
   first-class everywhere.

**No finding requires a baseline change.** Every proposed fix implements the
approved docs more faithfully.

## 3. Findings

Format: **ID · Title — Severity.** Where / evidence / baseline / cause /
proposed fix.

### Blockers

**F-01 · Phone layouts split into two squeezed columns; status chips clipped — Blocker**
- **Where:** Business Home, Jobs list, Operator Work, My Jobs, Vehicles,
  Landing, Audit filters, High-value details (8 grids), plus 7 `sm:flex-row`
  layouts. 15 files.
- **Evidence:**
  - `business_sw_jobs-list@360.png`: two ~150px columns; status chips cut off
    ("Imethibi…", "Imepang…"), so the job's state is unreadable.
  - `operator_en_work@360.png`: a single card squeezed into half the screen.
  - Measured horizontal overflow at 360px: Home and Jobs list 18px (en) and
    43px (sw); My Jobs 11px and 28px.
- **Baseline:** the P4/P5B breakpoint token sets `sm` = **360px**
  (`tokens.ts:298`). P3 §24.2 specifies a single column on mobile. P3 §24.3
  says no horizontal body scroll.
- **Cause:** the code uses `sm:` as if it were Tailwind's default 640px. On
  this project it fires at the smallest phone.
- **Fix (within baseline):** move multi-column layouts to `md:` (600) or
  `lg:` (900). Add a lint or test guard against `sm:grid-cols-*`.

**F-02 · Business "Cancel request" is one tap: no confirmation, no reason, no late-cancel warning — Blocker**
- **Where:** Business Job Detail, every cancellable state including
  ASSIGNED and AT_PICKUP (`business_en_job-at-pickup-elevated@360.png`).
- **Cause:** `JobDetailPage.tsx` calls
  `cancel.mutate('BUSINESS_CHANGED_MIND')` on click. The reason is hardcoded,
  and there's no dialog.
- **Baseline:** P3 §6.4 says "Cancel (with reason; consequence screen if a
  late-cancel — §23)". P2 correction 4: a cancel after ASSIGNED and before
  AT_PICKUP is a *late cancellation*, and a cancel after AT_PICKUP is a
  wasted trip. Three in 30 days means an admin-review flag for that side.
- **Why Blocker:** it's irreversible and it has a consequence (it counts
  toward the business's flag), yet it's reachable with a single accidental
  tap.
- **Fix (within baseline):** a confirm sheet with a reason picker (the
  existing `CancellationReason` codes) and optional text. When the job is at
  or after ASSIGNED, show the late-cancel / wasted-trip consequence line
  before confirming. Reuse the Increment 8 staff cancel modal pattern.

**F-03 · The driver gets the business's screen and wording, not the §10.1 "Current Job" home — Blocker**
- **Where:** Driver on Job Detail
  (`operator_sw_job-at-pickup-elevated-driver@360.png`).
- **Evidence:**
  - The state line addressed to the driver says "Dereva anachukua bidhaa
    zako" ("The driver is collecting **your** goods"). That is the business's
    line.
  - No "call the pickup contact" and no "Go to pickup" (map hand-off).
  - Full 9-step timeline, price and cargo cards come before anything
    driver-relevant.
  - 10 wrapped nav links sit above it all.
- **Baseline:** P3 §10.1 information hierarchy: (1) job ref + state line
  (2) **one** next action, large (3) pickup → destination **with the relevant
  contact for this step** (4) cargo (5) vehicle (6) a **compact 3-step
  progress**. The secondary action is "Call pickup/recipient contact". P3
  §24.1 says the driver screen is action-dominant, with desktop capped at
  ~640px.
- **Why Blocker:** on a phone, at the pickup gate, the driver can't call the
  pickup contact from the app, and the copy talks to the wrong person.
- **Fix (within baseline):** a driver rendering of Job Detail per §10.1 (or
  a dedicated Current Job route), with driver-voiced state lines (add
  `jobs:statusLine.*_DRIVER` keys) and the step contact with a `tel:` link.
  **Resolved by the founder (2026-09-23):** once assigned, the driver sees
  both the sender's (pickup) and the receiver's (recipient) name and phone.

### Majors

**F-04 · One flat, wrapping top bar for every role instead of the role-specific navigation — Major**
- **Evidence:**
  - A business owner sees 8 links on a phone, the operator 10 and Ops 13,
    wrapping to 3–4 lines, including Diagnostics, Groups and Operator for a
    business.
  - On desktop there's no sidebar and no persistent "Request transport"
    (`business_en_home@1280.png`).
  - On `/ops/*` the header stays in the 768px column while the content is
    1200px (`ops_en_ops-overview@1280.png`).
- **Baseline:** P1 IA (six primary-nav sets). P3 §24.2: mobile uses a
  role-specific **bottom tab bar** plus "More", desktop a persistent left
  sidebar. The Design Phase 6 plan §4 (`RoleTabBar` / `RoleSidebar`) was
  deferred in Increment 4.
- **Fix (within baseline):** build `RoleTabBar` (mobile) and `RoleSidebar`
  (desktop) from the P1 per-role nav matrix. Diagnostics moves to staff-only
  or dev-only.

**F-05 · Swahili isn't first-class in shared UI: English leaks through — Major**
- **Evidence:**
  - **"(Required)"** in English on every required field in the Swahili UI
    (`business_sw_create-job@360.png`). `Field` defaults
    `requiredText = 'Required'`, and none of its 7 callers pass a
    translation.
  - The connectivity label **"Synced"** is English on every screen:
    `CONNECTIVITY_VISUAL.labelEn`, with no label passed.
  - Also untranslated:
    - `JobStatusHeader` "· as of"
    - the Modal close button's `aria-label="Close"`
    - "Skip to content"
    - `ErrorBoundary` "Something went wrong"
    - the `NextActionCard` default empty label
    - workspace labels "Operations" / "Platform Admin"
  - **The login page has no language switcher** (`public_sw_login@360.png`).
    It sits outside `AppShell`, so a Swahili-first user can't switch before
    signing in.
- **Baseline:** `design-brief.md` voice (EN and SW both first-class). P3 §24.3
  requires the language switcher in the app bar everywhere.
- **Fix (within baseline):** remove the English defaults from shared
  components, make them translation-driven, and add the switcher to
  `/login`. The existing en/sw key-parity test doesn't catch literals, so add
  a lint rule for JSX string literals in `components/`.

**F-06 · "Synced" overstates what the app knows — Major**
- **Evidence:** the green "● Synced" in every app bar. The indicator only
  reflects `navigator.onLine`, and there's no sync engine (Design Phase 6
  plan §3.2).
- **Baseline:** P3 §23 says offline vs server-confirmed must never be
  blurred. "Synced" implies the data on screen is confirmed current.
- **Fix (within baseline):** show "Online" / "Offline" (translated) until a
  real sync engine exists. Reserve "Synced" / "Syncing…" for that phase.

**F-07 · Small (36px) buttons used as main actions, including on phones — Major**
- **Evidence:** `size="compact"` is used 27 times, e.g. Post update, Show
  contact details, staff Cancel, incident admin actions, Business "Cancel
  request", filter chips. Several are the main action of their section, on
  mobile.
- **Baseline:** P4 §475: compact 36px is "desktop dense tables **only**;
  never a primary". P3 §24.3 / P4 §994: at least 44px targets, at least 56px
  on driver-critical actions.
- **Fix (within baseline):** default size outside dense desktop tables.
  Keep `compact` for table-row actions at `lg:` and up.

**F-08 · Job pages don't say which job you're on — Major**
- **Evidence:**
  - Business, operator and staff Job Detail show no job reference and no
    page heading (`business_en_job-at-pickup-elevated@360.png`,
    `ops_en_job-staff-high@360.png`).
  - `JobDetailPage`, `CreateJobPage` and `PickupConfirmPage` have no `<h1>`,
    and `JobStatusHeader` renders no heading.
  - The staff view also omits the business name and the value band, which
    drives what staff may do (F-02 / ADR-2D-33) and whether a high-value
    review is pending.
- **Baseline:** P3 §10.1 / §6.4 hierarchy begins "(1) Job ref + state line".
  WCAG 1.3.1 / 2.4.6 (headings).
- **Fix (within baseline):** `JobStatusHeader` gets an `<h1>` with the
  reference, plus route summary and business. The staff variant adds band
  and high-value status.

**F-09 · Business Home offers "Confirm completion", which can't be done — Major**
- **Evidence:** `business_en_home@1280.png`, "5536CA … Confirm completion".
  No manual DELIVERED → COMPLETED endpoint exists (completion is automatic,
  `jobHelpers.businessNextAction` doc). The detail page then says it
  completes automatically.
- **Fix (within baseline):** show it as a status ("Delivered — completes
  automatically on {date}"), not an action, and drop it from "Needs your
  action".

**F-10 · Recipient code entry has no visible or accessible label — Major**
- **Evidence:** `recipient_en_recipient-confirm@360.png`: six empty boxes
  with no instruction. axe rates `label` **critical**, with 6 unlabelled
  inputs (en and sw).
- **Baseline:** P3 §20.2 ("enter the code we sent by SMS"). WCAG 1.3.1 /
  3.3.2 / 4.1.2. This is the **only** screen a recipient ever uses.
- **Fix (within baseline):** a visible label and helper text ("Enter the
  6-digit code sent to your phone"), per-cell `aria-label`s ("Digit 1 of 6"),
  and a "didn't get it?" hint. Also fix the lower-case "fikisha" wordmark
  in `RecipientHeader`.

**F-11 · The offer history says "Expired" for offers that were replaced — Major**
- **Evidence:** `business_en_negotiation@360.png`. The business's posted
  price and the operator's first offer both read "Expired", directly above
  the operator's "Accepted". The effective status is SUPERSEDED, not
  expired, and it reads as contradictory at a money decision.
- **Fix (within baseline, copy):** "Replaced by a newer offer" (EN) and a
  Swahili equivalent (to be reviewed by a native speaker), with a lighter
  visual weight than active offers.

**F-12 · Pickup and delivery proof method choice is too small and unclear for drivers — Major**
- **Evidence:** `operator_sw_pickup-proof-elevated@360.png`. The two methods
  are small pill buttons, nothing is pre-selected, and there is no primary
  action until one is tapped.
- **Baseline:** P3 §11.1–11.3 (method selection, band matrix). P4 at least
  56px driver-critical targets. P3 §10.1 "one next action, large".
- **Fix (within baseline):** full-width 56px option rows with a one-line
  explanation each ("The sender reads you a code" / "The sender confirms in
  the app"), and the most common method pre-selected.

**F-13 · Inconsistent form controls, two with accessibility errors — Major**
- **Evidence:**
  - 6 raw `<textarea>`s with three different heights (60, 72 and 80px)
  - 8 raw `<select>`s, each styled locally
  - axe **critical** `select-name`: an unlabelled `<select>` on Groups
    (`operator_en_groups@1280.png`)
  - axe **serious** `definition-list`: malformed `<dl>` on High-value review
- **Fix (within baseline):** `Textarea` and `Select` primitives, built to the
  P4 input spec (44px, `line.strong`, focus ring, label wiring), then
  replace the raw ones.

### Minors

**F-14 · Dates and times ignore the app language and EAT — Minor**
- **Evidence:** 12 ad-hoc formatters: `toLocaleString()` /
  `toLocaleTimeString(undefined, …)`. They use the browser's locale and
  timezone, not the chosen language or East Africa Time. Timeline rows show
  only HH:MM with no date, and Ops shows seconds.
- **Baseline:** P3 §24.3 "EAT times". P3 §14.1 timestamp (EAT).
- **Fix:** one `formatDateTime` / `formatRelative` helper
  (`Intl.DateTimeFormat` with the i18n language and `Africa/Nairobi`). Ops
  queues show age ("3 h ago").

**F-15 · Ops queues don't say why an item is there — Minor**
- **Evidence:** `ops_en_ops-overview@1280.png`. "Jobs needing attention"
  mixes disputed, failed and stale jobs with no reason shown and raw
  timestamps. The search placeholder is forced to upper case ("E.G.
  7F3A2C").
- **Baseline:** P3 §18.1: "each row's name summarises the item **and its
  age**".
- **Fix:** a reason chip per row (Stale 26h / Disputed / Couldn't complete)
  and relative age. Only the input value is uppercased, not the placeholder.

**F-16 · A dev label shows on every screen — Minor**
- **Evidence:** the footer "Fikisha · Phase 2A foundation", on every
  screenshot.
- **Fix:** remove it, or show a dev-only build tag.

**F-17 · Duplicate and unnecessary API calls on load — Minor**
- **Evidence:**
  - Signed-out pages call `/me` and `/auth/refresh` **twice** each.
  - For users with no operator profile, `/operators/me` 404s are re-fetched
    on nearly every screen, logged as console errors (16 on the business
    walkthrough).
- **Fix:** cache the "no operator profile" result for the session
  (`staleTime: Infinity` on 404). Check the double auth bootstrap (it may be
  React StrictMode in dev only; verify before changing anything).

### Polish

**F-18 · A literal "·" in link text — Polish**
- "· Report an issue" / "· Ripoti tatizo". P3 uses `·` as *notation* for a
  secondary action, and it was copied into the visible copy.
- **Fix:** drop the dot.
